Commander Tower

Delete your account

From the app

  1. Open Commander Tower and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

If you own a group, the app asks you to delete it or hand it over to another member first, so the other members do not lose their history without warning.

By email

If you cannot open the app, write to commander.tower.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account and ask for its deletion. We will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and password) and your profile (name and avatar).
  • Your decks and their card lists, your card collection, wishlists and trades.
  • Your membership of every group.

What is kept

Games already saved in a group stay in that group's history so the other members keep their results. Your seat in those games is kept without a link to an account. Nothing else is retained.
